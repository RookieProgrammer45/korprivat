# Australian driving schools

Each row in `schools.json` is a car driving school whose own website states a single-lesson price in Australian dollars. The price, email and phone were read from that page on 2026-10-28. A package total or a "from" price is not stored as the lesson price.

The listing is advertised and contact-only. It is not a verified permit, and DriveLinkUp does not book the lesson.

- Sydney: Drive Matrix, 1 hour automatic day lesson AUD 80
- Melbourne: DOS Driving School, 1 hour automatic lesson AUD 65; Auslink Driving Academy, 60 minute lesson AUD 75
- Brisbane: UNO Driving School AUD 75; Ace Driving School AUD 90; Briswide Driving School AUD 93
- Toowoomba: U Can Drive, 1 hour lesson AUD 90
- Perth: Acme Driving School, 1 hour lesson AUD 80
- Adelaide: My Driving Trainer, 60 minute lesson AUD 110; Excel Drive, 60 minute lesson AUD 121
- Hobart: Eastside Driving, 1 hour lesson AUD 80
