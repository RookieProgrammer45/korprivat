# UK driving schools

Each row in `schools.json` is a car driving school whose own website states a single-lesson price in pounds. The price, email and phone were read from that page on 2026-10-28. A package total or a "from" price is not stored as the lesson price.

The listing is advertised and contact-only. It is not a verified permit, and DriveLinkUp does not book the lesson.
