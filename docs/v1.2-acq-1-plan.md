# ACQ-1 plan — Claim funnel

**Authority:** specs/09 §13 ACQ-1 and SC-21. Strategy: docs/acquisition-strategy.md §4 and §6.  
**Status:** pull request open, not merged — [#35](https://github.com/RookieProgrammer45/korprivat/pull/35) on `feat/acq-1-claim-funnel`.  
**Flag:** `CLAIM_FUNNEL` defaults off. While off, prospect profile and claim routes respond 404 and do not query.

## What this phase does

- Prospects are private until eligibility and data-quality checks both pass. A passing listing does not require a completed claim, and it has no booking surfaces.
- Teaching categories on a prospect are A, B, C, and D. They are not commercial authorization scopes.
- The only accepted import source is `SYNTHETIC_FIXTURE`. A register scrape is refused.
- A claim records identity and mandate references for manual review. Verified identity, mandate, and representative authority still grant no scopes and write no authorization events.
- An imported legacy cancellation tier is stored as a draft policy object. It is not an active policy.

## Out of this phase

Real register collection, bulk publication, outreach, school-mediated instructor onboarding, the lead dashboard, and category offering publication.
