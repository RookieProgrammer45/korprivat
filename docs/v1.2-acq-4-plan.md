# ACQ-4 plan — Category offerings

**Authority:** specs/09 §13 ACQ-4 and SC-22.  
**Status:** pull request open, not merged — [#38](https://github.com/RookieProgrammer45/korprivat/pull/38) on `feat/acq-4-category-offerings`.  
**Flag:** `CATEGORY_OFFERINGS` defaults off. While off, offering publication and booking creation do not read teaching permissions.

## What this phase does

- A teaching permission is a stored fact: category, legal entity, location, and evidence. The offering's category tag is checked against that fact.
- A school whose current permission is B at its entity and location cannot publish A, C, or D.
- An offering can select only that listing's active approved cancellation policy. The booking stores a copy of the category and policy version.
- `/kategori/[category]` and `GET /api/categories/[category]` stay hidden until the flag is on. The page has no booking surface.

## Out of this phase

Concierge prefill, outreach, and a second active cancellation policy per listing.
