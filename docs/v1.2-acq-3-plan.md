# ACQ-3 plan — School-mediated instructors

**Authority:** specs/09 §13 ACQ-3 and SC-23. Strategy: docs/acquisition-strategy.md §2 segment C.  
**Status:** pull request open, not merged — [#36](https://github.com/RookieProgrammer45/korprivat/pull/36) on `feat/acq-3-school-instructors`.  
**Flag:** `SCHOOL_MEDIATED_INSTRUCTORS` defaults off. While off, instructor publication, offerings, and availability keep the current path and do not read affiliation rows.

## What this phase does

- A school representative invites an instructor. Teaching categories must be within the school listing's categories. Inventory delegation defaults off.
- Accepting the invitation records a verified affiliation and grants no commercial scopes.
- With the flag on, an instructor without that affiliation cannot publish a listing, sell an offering, or open a native diary. A diary opens only when the school delegated inventory.
- An instructor cannot grant scopes. A verified school representative still can.

## Out of this phase

Outreach, the lead dashboard, category offering publication, and independent selling. Independent selling stays a legal decision outside this build.
